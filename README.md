# Safety Headmap: AI for resident safety and reporting.

Client: City of San José, police and crime
Team: James Doan, Eric Liao, Taran Sahota, Rithvik Saladi, Aug–Oct 2026
My part: Brainstormed heatmap design and realistic implementations, designed team logo, set up presentation slides, and presented demo.

## The problem
San Jose residents and UPD crime analysts lack a clear, timely way to turn scattered incident reports into location-based patterns, making it difficult to identify where safety incidents are clustering around San Jose.

## What we built
[Three bullets: what the prototype does, what the user sees, what a human still decides.]
- Shows residents areas of high amounts of incidents for them to avoid the area or report.
- The user sees a form to input information or just to see the heatmap of where to avoid walking.
- A human still decides to approve or deny the reports based on authenticity and redundance.

## What I would do next
Where it breaks: Multiple different users could submit different reports about the same incident, leading to an artificially inflated incident hotspot.
Solution: The analyst reviewer can mark reports as a duplicate in the review process. Additionally, the analyst can remove records from the heat map if they find this out later.
